I was surprised and honored to receive the 2025 COES Education Award, along with Dr. Blake Farman, for our work on the "Construction Zone Compound" and the AXIOM (Applied eXplorations In Open Mathematics) Lab.

{% include figure.html path="assets/img/news/COESEducation.jpeg" alt="Ann and Dr. Blake Farman holding their 2025 COES Education Award trophies, standing with a college administrator." class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

The Construction Zone Compound was a community built mathematical art installation designed and organized in collaboration with Studio Infinity. Check out [Dr. Farman's write-up](https://www.blakefarman.phd/news/construction_zone_compound/) for some great pictures!
