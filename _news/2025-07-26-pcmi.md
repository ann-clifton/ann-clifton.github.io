I spent three weeks (July 6&ndash;26, 2025) in Park City, Utah, attending the Institute for Advanced Study's Park City Mathematics Institute Graduate Summer School. The topic for 2025 was "Extremal and Probabilistic Combinatorics." Each week there were three minicourses taught by leading experts along with research seminars and cross program lectures.

It was incredible being immersed in research and learning in such a beautiful location.
