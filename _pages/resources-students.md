## Summer and Research Opportunities

Most summer programs pay a stipend and cover housing and travel. Applications usually open in the fall, and many deadlines fall between January and March, so start looking early. If you would like help choosing programs or want someone to read your application materials, come talk to me.

### Where to search

- [AMS Opportunities](https://www.ams.org/opportunities) — The American Mathematical Society's listing of programs, fellowships, and awards. You can filter by audience (for example, undergraduate students) and by program type; "Research Experience for Undergraduates" is one of the options.
- [MathPrograms.org](https://www.mathprograms.org/) — The application site that many math REUs and summer programs use. You can browse the open programs and submit most of your applications in one place.
- [Steve Butler's Math REU list](https://sites.google.com/view/mathreu) — A list of math REU programs kept up to date by Steve Butler at Iowa State, with dates and deadlines.
- [NSF REU site search (mathematical sciences)](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/reu/search?f%5b0%5d=reu_research_area:25741) — Every REU site funded by the National Science Foundation in the mathematical sciences. Change the research area to see sites in other fields. NSF-funded REUs are open to U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, and permanent residents.
- [Pathways to Science](https://www.pathwaystoscience.org/) — A searchable database of summer research programs, scholarships, and fellowships across all of STEM.

### Research programs

- [Fields Undergraduate Summer Research Program (FUSRP)](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/activities/26-27/FUSRP) — Small teams of students work with a faculty supervisor at the Fields Institute in Toronto or a partner university during June and July. Open to students from around the world.
- [Raider REU at Middle Tennessee State University](https://urc.mtsu.edu/raider-reu/) — An eight-week, fully funded summer REU in STEM education research, for STEM majors and future STEM teachers who are interested in how people learn.
- [Polymath Jr.](https://geometrynyc.wixsite.com/polymathreu) — A large online summer research program in which students work in teams on open problems. Because it is remote, it works well if you cannot travel for the summer.
- [SMALL at Williams College](https://hub.williams.edu/math/small/) — A nine-week residential program, and one of the largest math REUs in the country.
- [MSRI-UP](https://www.slmath.org/msri-up) — A summer research program at the Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute in Berkeley, California.
- [Summer@ICERM](https://icerm.brown.edu/summerug/) — An eight-week research program at Brown University's math institute, with a different topic each summer.
- [RIPS at IPAM](https://www.ipam.ucla.edu/programs/student-research-programs/) — Research in Industrial Projects for Students at UCLA. Teams work on problems posed by companies and national labs.

### Summer schools and semester programs

- [PCMI Undergraduate Summer School](https://www.ias.edu/pcmi) — A three-week residential program in Park City, Utah with courses for undergraduates at different levels, alongside researchers, graduate students, and teachers.
- [Budapest Semesters in Mathematics](https://budapestsemesters.com/) — A semester or summer of mathematics courses in Budapest, Hungary, taught in English.
- [EDGE](https://www.edgeforwomen.org/) — A summer program for women who are about to start a Ph.D. program in the mathematical sciences.

### Government and national lab internships

- [DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli) — Paid research internships at Department of Energy national laboratories, offered in summer, fall, and spring.
- [NIST Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)](https://www.nist.gov/surf) — Summer research at the National Institute of Standards and Technology.
- [NSA student programs](https://www.intelligencecareers.gov/nsa/students-and-internships) — Summer internships in mathematics, statistics, and computer science at the National Security Agency. These need a security clearance, so the deadlines are early in the fall.
